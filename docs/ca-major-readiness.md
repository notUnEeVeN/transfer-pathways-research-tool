### Computer Science — the one to present, but its templates are the least complete

Accurate enough to present, and the only major whose bachelor templates a
person built from the catalog rather than reviewed after the fact. The
weaknesses are structural rather than evidential:

- **Four documents fail a structural check**, all small: UCLA states 181 units
  against a 180-unit degree, Riverside 182/180, Merced 125/120, and Santa Cruz
  carries 57 units of general education against a 51-unit certification
  pattern.
- **Why we suspect what they are:** the three over-totals are 1–5 units, which
  is the signature of one requirement counted in two groups, not of a wrong
  template. Santa Cruz's GE excess is the campus's *native* GE requirement —
  a certified transfer never faces it, so it should not sit in the transfer
  denominator.
- **No CS template records a catalog year, unit system, GE model or
  upper-division floor.** These are the oldest documents we have, written
  before the standard existed. Biology and Economics record all of them.
- Associate side is the thinner half: 27 of 45 local A.S. and 31 of 69 A.S.-T
  records carry a verification verdict.

### Biology — structurally the cleanest, weaker provenance

- **Zero structural failures.** Every template closes its units and resolves
  its section tiers.
- All nine carry a signed verification verdict — Tybalt Mallet on eight, Lucky
  Hong on one — dated 3–12 August 2026.
- **The `ai_researched_needs_human_verification` label on all nine is stale.**
  The verification record supersedes it, and the figures already say so
  ("9 stale pre-verification research-status labels remain"). Worth correcting
  the label rather than explaining it every time.
- The honest caveat is provenance, not structure: these templates were
  assembled by AI web research and then reviewed, where CS was built by hand.
- Open modelling warnings: 6 record no upper-division floor, 5 carry an
  unstructured GE model, 5 have a choice group with no reachable option, 1 is
  under-modelled.

### Economics — same structure, but a different credential entirely

- **Zero structural failures.** Nine signed verdicts (Tybalt ×7, Lucky Hong
  ×2), same August window, same stale label.
- **There is no Economics local A.S. anywhere in California.** All 18
  local-A.S. records resolve to "not found". Its transfer credential is the
  A.A.-T/A.S.-T: 97 colleges, 55 verified, plus 23 other local awards.
- **Why this matters for any cross-major chart:** comparing Economics on a
  transfer degree against CS and Biology on a local A.S. compares two different
  credentials. Cross-major work has to pin A.S.-T/A.A.-T for all three.

### The caveat that applies to all three

The bachelor side is in good shape; **the associate side is where verification
is thin** — between 36% and 60% of found degrees carry a verdict, depending on
major and credential. Any figure showing all sourced records rather than
verified-only is exploratory, and the figures label it that way.
