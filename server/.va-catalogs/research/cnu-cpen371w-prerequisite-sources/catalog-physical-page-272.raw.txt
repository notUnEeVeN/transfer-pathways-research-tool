Major in Cybersecurity
Cybersecurity focuses on the protection of computing
systemsincludinghardware,software,anddatafromdigital
attacks. The cybersecurity major provides students with
a strong foundation in computer science and information
science. Building on this foundation of knowledge,
students then study specific topics and techniques related
to cybersecurity. Students end their studies with the
cybersecurity capstone, an opportunity to explore and
develop a cybersecurity-related project.
