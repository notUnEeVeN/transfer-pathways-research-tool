IST Course Resources

There are many useful resources on this page that can be used to help students review concepts that are covered in IT courses such as computer science, networking, programming, and more.

We encourage you to attend focused study groups that are facilitated by tutors throughout the year. If you would like to schedule an appointment with a tutor, you can complete the Tutoring Appointment Request, visit or call one of our ACE offices, or stop by the ACE Online front desk to speak with a staff member.
