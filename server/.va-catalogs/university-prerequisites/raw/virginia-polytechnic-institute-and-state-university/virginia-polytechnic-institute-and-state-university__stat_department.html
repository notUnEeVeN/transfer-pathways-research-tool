Overview

Statistics is the science of learning from data. Statisticians develop and apply methods for collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and communicating information to solve problems across science, business, government, industry, healthcare, and technology. As data become increasingly central to decision-making, statistics graduates are in high demand for careers requiring analytical, computational, and problem-solving skills.

Students in Virginia Tech’s statistics program gain hands-on experience through coursework, undergraduate research, internships, and experiential learning opportunities. The department encourages students to connect classroom learning with practical applications through collaborations with faculty, industry, and research groups across the university.

Statistics majors graduate prepared for careers in a wide range of fields, including data science, actuarial science, finance, government, technology, healthcare, research, and education. The program also provides excellent preparation for graduate study in statistics, data science, biostatistics, analytics, and related disciplines.

The department also offers M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in statistics https://catalog.vt.edu/graduate/degree-programs/statistics/#text.

Bachelor of Science in Statistics

The Department of Statistics offers a Bachelor of Science in Statistics with two degree options: Statistical Data Science and Statistical Methods and Theory.

Statistical Data Science Option

The Statistical Data Science option emphasizes modern statistical modeling, computing, machine learning, and data analytics. Students develop strong programming and computational skills while learning to work with complex real-world data. This option is designed for students interested in careers in data science, analytics, technology, finance, government, consulting, and related fields.

Statistical Methods and Theory Option

The Statistical Methods and Theory option emphasizes the mathematical foundations of statistics, including probability, statistical theory, and advanced quantitative reasoning. This option is well suited for students interested in graduate study or theoretically focused careers.

The Statistical Applications and Innovations Group

Associated with the Department, the Statistical Applications and Innovations Group (SAIG) provides assistance for research projects to participating members of the University community and outside organizations. Statistics Department faculty members and students collaborate to design studies, analyze data, and interpret results for Virginia Tech affiliated clients and external clients in business, industry, government, and non-profit organizations. SAIG provides both experiential learning for statistics students and service to the University and beyond. To learn more, visit https://saig.stat.vt.edu/.

STAT 1004 - The First Year Experience in Learning from Data (2 credits) 
Introduction to the field of statistics and aspects of college life for first year students. Recognize ethical issues and vulnerabilities in analyses when learning from data and extrapolating to large populations.
