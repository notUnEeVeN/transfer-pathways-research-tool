This is the preliminary (or launch) version of the 2026-2027 VCU Bulletin. Courses that expose students to cutting-edge content and transformative learning may be added and notification of additional program approvals may be received prior to finalization. General education program content is also subject to change. The final edition and full PDF version will include these updates and will be available in August prior to the beginning of the fall semester.

PHYZ 101. Foundations of Physics Laboratory. 1 Hour.

Semester course; 2 laboratory hours. 1 credit. Corequisite: PHYS 101. An optional laboratory consisting of experiments and activities correlated with PHYS 101.

PHYZ 103. Elementary Astronomy Laboratory. 1 Hour.

Semester course; 2 laboratory hours. 1 credit. Concurrent prerequisite: PHYS 103. An optional laboratory course consisting of experiments and activities related to PHYS 103.

PHYZ 207. University Physics I Laboratory. 1 Hour.

Semester course; 2 laboratory hours. 1 credit. Prerequisite: MATH 151. Concurrent prerequisite: PHYS 207. An algebra-based laboratory course correlated with PHYS 207.

PHYZ 208. University Physics II Laboratory. 1 Hour.

Semester course; 2 laboratory hours. 1 credit. Prerequisites: MATH 200, PHYS 207 and PHYZ 207. Concurrent prerequisite: PHYS 208. An algebra-based physics lab course correlated with PHYS 208.

PHYZ 320. Modern Physics Laboratory. 1 Hour.

Semester course; 3 laboratory hours. 1 credit. Concurrent prerequisite: PHYS 320. Experimental work correlated with PHYS 320.
