SPA 1030 - Accelerated Elementary Spanish

Credit(s) 3

In a global world, exposure to and familiarity with a foreign language and culture is an important skill, both in the job market and in our everyday lives. This accelerated course introduces students not only to the basics of communication (speaking, writing, reading, and listening) in Spanish, but also to the diversity and influence of Hispanic culture worldwide.

Course Frequency:
Fall, Spring
